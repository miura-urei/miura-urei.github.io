Research

Questions at the intersection of condensed matter, statistical physics, and mathematical physics.

How do microscopic structures give rise to collective behavior?

Supersymmetry & quantum matter

I study supersymmetry in interacting lattice models, including its spontaneous breaking, emergent spacetime symmetry, and the structure of quantum phases.

Kitaev chains · Nicolai models · Quantum criticality

Nonequilibrium dynamics

I investigate how constraints and fragmentation shape steady states, relaxation, and metastability in stochastic many-body systems.

Exclusion processes · Exact steady states · Relaxation

Geometry, topology & integrability

My recent work explores connections between integrable dynamics, Krylov geometry, counterdiabatic transport, and topological flat bands.

Toda–Arnoldi correspondence · Nonlinear dynamics · Topology
